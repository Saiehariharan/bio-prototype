The Problem

Genetic data is permanent, sensitive, and highly valuable.

Why it matters

Genetic information is deeply personal and cannot be changed like a password. If an authorized account is compromised, the exposure may be long-lasting and difficult to contain.

Limitations of traditional security

Password checks alone are not enough. A valid login does not guarantee a safe user action. Suspicious behavior and abnormal data access patterns must also be detected in real time.
